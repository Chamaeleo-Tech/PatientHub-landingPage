01 About PatientHub

PatientHub is a digital clinic management platform developed by Chamaeleo Tech (Beirut, Lebanon). It is designed to help doctors, clinics, and medical staff manage patients, appointments, billing, prescriptions, dental charts, forms, staff access, and related administrative tasks.

PatientHub does not provide medical services, medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations. All medical decisions remain the sole responsibility of the licensed healthcare provider.

04 Platform Services

PatientHub provides the following features and services, subject to your subscription plan:

  • Appointment Management: Create, schedule, and track patient appointments via a calendar view. View daily, weekly, and monthly schedules.
  • Patient Management: Maintain complete patient profiles including medical history, contact details, form submissions, prescriptions, billing records, and dental charts.
  • Billing & Invoicing: Generate invoices, track payment status, monitor revenue KPIs, and view financial performance reports per clinic.
  • Dental Chart: Record per-tooth treatments, clinical notes, and treatment history using an interactive dental chart interface.
  • Digital Prescriptions: Issue and manage digital prescriptions per patient, including medication, dosage, frequency, and prescribing doctor.
  • Forms & Workflows: Build custom intake forms, questionnaires, and patient report templates. Collect patient responses before or during visits.
  • WhatsApp Integration: Send automated appointment reminders, booking confirmations, and clinic notifications to patients via WhatsApp Business.
  • Google Calendar Sync: Synchronize appointments with Google Calendar via OAuth authorization for individual users.
  • Expense Tracking: Log and categorize clinic expenses and monitor financial performance alongside revenue.
  • Multi-Clinic Management: Manage multiple clinic locations under a single account, with separate staff assignments, schedules, and billing per clinic.
  • Staff & Role Management: Add and manage clinic staff (doctors, secretaries, administrators) with fine-grained, module-level permissions.
  • Public Booking Page: Each doctor may have a shareable public booking link allowing patients to self-schedule appointments online.
  • Clinic Directory: Clinics may publish a branded public directory page listing their doctors and enabling online booking.
  • To-Do List: Manage daily tasks and reminders for clinic staff.
  • Activity Tracking: Audit logs track significant user actions within the platform for security and accountability.
  • PWA Support: PatientHub can be installed as a Progressive Web App on mobile and desktop devices for offline-capable access.

Features may be updated, modified, or discontinued at any time. We will provide notice of significant changes where reasonably possible.
